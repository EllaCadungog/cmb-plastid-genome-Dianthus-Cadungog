## Question 4: Annotated Gene Content and IR Duplication

**tRNA Genes:** 34  
**rRNA Genes:** 6  

**Pseudogenes / Gene Losses:**  
The plastome contains a partial **ycf1** region at the IRb/SSC boundary. The IR region also contributes to gene duplication because genes located within the IR are represented in two copies.

**Why Do IR Genes Appear in Two Copies?**

The inverted repeat regions are two identical or nearly identical DNA sequences arranged in opposite orientations. Genes located within these regions are therefore duplicated, with one copy occurring in IRa and another copy occurring in IRb.
