## Reference Notes

The *Dianthus caryophyllus* chloroplast genome has a typical quadripartite structure consisting of the LSC, SSC, and two IR regions. The NC_039650.1 plastome is 149,604 bp long and has an overall GC content of approximately 36.30%. The LSC, SSC, and IR regions are approximately 84,774 bp, 17,100 bp, and 22,865 bp, respectively. The IR regions have a higher GC content than the LSC and SSC regions. 

The genome contains approximately 83 protein-coding genes, 34 tRNA genes, and rRNA genes associated with the duplicated IR regions. Several genes contain introns, including rps16, atpF, rpoC1, petB, petD, rpl16, ndhB, ndhA, ycf1, ycf3, and clpP. The rps12 gene is produced through trans-splicing.
